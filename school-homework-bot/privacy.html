Privacy Policy

School Homework Bot

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. About the application

School Homework Bot is a personal, non-commercial educational assistant intended for private family use. The application is currently under development.

2. Google user data

With the user's authorization, the application accesses educational information through the official Google Classroom API.

The information may include:

  • Course names and identifiers.
  • Homework titles and descriptions.
  • Publication dates and assignment deadlines.
  • Links to assignments and educational materials.

Access to Google Classroom is read-only. The application does not modify assignments, grades or student submissions.

3. Purpose of data use

Educational information is used to organize school schedules and homework and to present relevant information to authorized family members through Telegram.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising, profiling or AI model training.

4. Data storage and processing

During development, authorization credentials are stored locally on the developer's computer.

The planned production architecture uses Render for application hosting, restricted-access Google Sheets for educational records, and Telegram for delivery of homework information to authorized family members.

Google user data will be processed by these services only as necessary to provide the application's educational features.

Educational records and authorization credentials must not be published on public websites or in public source code repositories.

5. Data protection

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 and encrypted HTTPS connections. Authorization credentials must be stored separately from public source code.

6. Data retention and deletion

Educational information is intended to be retained only as long as needed for family educational organization.

Users may contact the developer to request deletion of stored educational information and authorization credentials.

Access to Google data can also be revoked through the user's Google Account settings:

Manage Google Account connections

School Homework Bot is designed to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
